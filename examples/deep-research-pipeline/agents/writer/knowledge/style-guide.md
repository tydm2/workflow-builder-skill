# Style guide (built-in)

## Structure
1. **Executive summary** — 3–5 sentences: the question, the answer, the confidence.
2. **One section per research question**, titled after the question, in brief order.
3. **Sources** — the brief's citation list, verbatim, deduplicated.

## Tone
- Neutral, factual, active voice; no marketing adjectives.
- State confidence explicitly when evidence is thin ("the evidence is mixed", "one source claims").

## Length
- Default 800–1500 words unless the brief's length target says otherwise.

## Citation style
- Inline `[n]` after each claim; resolve all `[n]` in the Sources section.
- Never drop a citation from the brief; never add one the brief doesn't have.

## Readability
- Short paragraphs (≤4 sentences), one idea each.
- Use a bullet list where the brief gives a list of facts.
