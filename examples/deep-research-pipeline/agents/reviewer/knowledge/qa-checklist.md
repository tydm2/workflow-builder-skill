# QA checklist (built-in)

Apply every item before writing the review. Each blocker must cite: **line → rule → fix**.

## Coverage
- [ ] One section per research question in the brief, in order
- [ ] No brief question left unanswered
- [ ] Executive summary matches the body's conclusions

## Accuracy
- [ ] Every factual claim traces to a brief finding
- [ ] No claim in the draft that isn't in the brief
- [ ] Confidence statements match the brief's evidence strength

## Citations
- [ ] Every `[n]` resolves in the Sources list
- [ ] No invented, dropped, or renumbered citations
- [ ] Source dates preserved from the brief

## Structure & style
- [ ] Structure follows `style-guide.md` (exec summary → sections → sources)
- [ ] Tone is neutral; no marketing adjectives
- [ ] Length within the brief's target

## Verdict
- **pass** — zero blockers.
- **pass-with-notes** — nits only, no factual/citation issues.
- **blocked** — any accuracy or citation blocker.
